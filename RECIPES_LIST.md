## Image Generation Tips

### For Curry Images (1-10):
- Show vibrant curry in a traditional Thai bowl
- Include visible ingredients (coconut milk, herbs, vegetables, protein)
- Use authentic Thai presentation style
- Bright, appetizing colors matching curry type (green, red, yellow, etc.)
- Garnish with Thai basil, kaffir lime leaves, or fresh chilies

### For Stir-fry Images (11-20):
- High-heat cooking appearance (slight char, caramelization)
- Show ingredients mixed together in a wok or plate
- Include visible proteins and vegetables
- Garnish with fresh herbs
- Steam or cooking action if possible

### For Soup Images (21-30):
- Show in traditional Thai soup bowl
- Visible ingredients floating in clear or creamy broth
- Aromatic herbs visible (lemongrass, kaffir lime leaves)
- Fresh garnishes on top
- Steam rising if possible

### For Street Food Images (31-40):
- Casual, authentic street food presentation
- Show on traditional serving plates or banana leaves
- Include typical accompaniments (sticky rice, dipping sauces)
- Vibrant, fresh ingredients
- Appetizing, rustic style

### For Dessert Images (41-50):
- Beautiful, colorful presentation
- Show textures clearly (sticky, creamy, layered)
- Include garnishes (sesame seeds, coconut flakes)
- Bright, appealing colors
- Traditional Thai dessert serving style
